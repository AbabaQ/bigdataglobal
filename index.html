01 / WHO WE ARE

We turn connections into commercial momentum.

BIG DATA GLOBAL SERVICES LIMITED is an international human resources management company with expertise in supply chain and management consulting, specialising in business development service provision.

Through ABABA Business Development Service Provider (BDSP), we bring together SMEs, business leaders, investors, organisations and innovators who want to build ventures and partnerships across Africa and the wider world.

⌕

Market intelligence

Regular updates on market opportunities, country-specific news, recent developments and financing reports. Information is segmented by sector, industry, country, city and activity—including market entry and distributor or agent opportunities.

02
▤

Service business pipeline

Discover projects brought forward by members and partners, including software projects, with opportunities to invest in, partner with or learn more. DEAM supports the protection of project intellectual property and confidentiality.

03
✳

Entrepreneurs pack

Explore low-capital projects, ideas and ready-made businesses that can be established as add-on or standalone ventures. These may be built around equipment, technology or simple solutions and come with a basic business plan.

Access sales and distribution opportunities from members, UK-based and international companies and wider networks. Reliable introductions into preferred countries and sectors can reduce market-entry risk—often without international travel.

PROGRAMME 01FULLY FUNDED*

Small Business Leadership Programme (SBLP)

Designed to help African/global SMEs improve leadership skills and learn with peers. Participants develop strategic leadership skills to help boost business performance.

Delivered through a consortium of leading business schools and private investors, with funding from government departments (MDAs) for eligible organisations.

Make a training enquiry ↗
PROGRAMME 02FULLY FUNDED*

SME Peer Network Programme (PNP)

An online virtual group programme bringing business leaders together to discuss and manage challenges in their businesses.

Topics include Human Resources, Sales and Marketing, Finance and Performance Management. For small and medium-sized projects/businesses with five employees or more.

Cyber Security & Data Management for SMEs

£250 stated programme fee

AFRICAN NETWORKING ACROSS THE UK

Local chapters. Shared ambition.

Our UK network brings African business leaders and diaspora communities together to develop relationships, open markets and create opportunities.

SCOTLAND

Glasgow

The chapter draws on the city's business and education environment, including Glasgow Caledonian University and its ethnic minority network and GCU Nigerian Student Society.

WEST MIDLANDS

Birmingham

A culturally diverse chapter bringing together business leaders from many African countries and cities.

SCOTLAND

Aberdeen

A community with over 42 African countries represented, bringing diaspora business leaders together.

NORTH EAST

Middlesbrough

The Teesside group holds monthly business networking meetings and is led by a Network Director from Togo, West Africa.

SCOTLAND

Edinburgh

A developing chapter seeking partnerships with existing networks to open new markets and trade.

NORTH EAST

Newcastle

A community-led network recognising the North East's potential for business with Africa.

OXFORDSHIRE

Oxford

A newer chapter led by established business leaders with companies in the UK and Nigeria.

NORTH WEST

Manchester

The Manchester Africa Business Network is growing, connecting UK and African members.

GREATER LONDON

London

Working with members and affiliates to bring forward events and opportunities. Follow the events programme and social groups for updates.
